WEEKLY PASS

You will receive 80 diamonds on the day of purchase, and the extra 20 diamonds will be sent to the Vault, which you must log in to redeem.


In addition, you will be required to log in to the weekly pass page for 6 consecutive days to redeem a total of 120 extra diamonds, with 20 extra diamonds per day.


During the 7 days, you will earn a total of:

  • 220 Diamonds
  • 210 Starlight Scores
  • 7 Diamond Bundle Passes
